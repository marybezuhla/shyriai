<p>Shyriai is an offline trainer for piloting drones and ground robots, in Ukrainian and English, for kids and adults. The app has no internet permission: progress stays on the phone.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Six courses.</b> Camera drone, FPV drone, ground robot, plane, aerial survey and search, intercepting a training target.</li>
<li><b>Three modes.</b> Kids: crashing is impossible, playful tasks, a grown-ups' lock. Simple: the drone helps, with hints. Adult: inertia, wind, battery, FPV up to Acro.</li>
<li><b>Lessons and missions.</b> From easy to hard, with clear pass conditions. Missions are rescue and training only, with no combat details.</li>
<li><b>Controls.</b> On-screen sticks, a gamepad or a real radio over USB; Mode 2 or Mode 1. Three cameras: from the ground, behind the drone and from the craft itself.</li>
<li><b>The pilot's book.</b> 10 chapters with drawings and "Check yourself" questions, a glossary and a little book for kids.</li>
<li><b>Sunflowers, licences and badges</b> with the overall progress.</li>
<li><b>Weather and free flight,</b> light and dark themes, three text sizes.</li>
</ul>
<p>No accounts, ads or tracking. The trainer does not replace lessons with an instructor and gives no right to fly. Requires Android 7 or newer.</p>
