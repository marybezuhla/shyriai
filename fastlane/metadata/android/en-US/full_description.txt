Shyriai is a piloting trainer for Android: a camera drone, an FPV drone and a ground robot (UGV). For kids and adults.

<b>Three modes, each with its own progress</b>
• Kids: crashing is impossible, playful tasks, a light look and larger text, an optional grown-ups' lock.
• Simple: the drone helps you level out, with hints.
• Adult: full physics with inertia, wind and battery, FPV all the way to Acro mode.

<b>Lessons and missions</b>
Lessons from easy to hard, each with clear pass conditions. Missions use the same skills in a story: a search in a field, a gate course, cargo delivery, casualty evacuation. For kids: a kitten on a roof, hoops, a parcel for granny. Scenarios are rescue and training only, with no combat details.

<b>Controls</b>
On-screen sticks, a gamepad, or a real radio over USB. Three cameras: from the ground, behind, and from the vehicle itself.

<b>Theory</b>
Short cards: before you fly, battery safety, lost signal, flying rules in Ukraine.

<b>Privacy</b>
No internet, no accounts, no ads, no permissions that concern you or your phone. Progress stays on the phone.

The trainer does not replace lessons with an instructor and gives no right to fly.
